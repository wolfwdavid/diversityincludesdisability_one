### My So-Called Disabled Life Podcast
I'm Eman Rimawi-Doster, a Black and Palestinian woman with multiple disabilities, born and raised in NYC, with a smart mouth and hilarious stories to share! Join me for reflections, conversations with cool a$$ disabled folks, and a look into my colorful life. I hope you enjoy My So-called Disabled Life!
