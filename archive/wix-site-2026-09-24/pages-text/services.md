# Services

## Workshops and Trainings
For 26 years, Eman has been an exceptional facilitator and educator on equity and inclusion. She offered a sliding scaled individualized and interactive workshop to improve all environments that include or want to include Disabled people. Moreover, she adds intersectional realms of information that support and guide businesses, organizations, schools, hospitals, corporations, and any and all places where disabled people go, because they are in every aspect of society.

## Modeling and Representation
As a Black and Palestinian woman with lupus, who is also a double amputee, she represents multiple marginalized communities. Having accurate representation in ads will not only increase your business but it will also encourage more people with disabilities to buy your products and promote them.
Image description: Eman is standing in a red room, looking up. She's wearing a black dress, showing off her shoulders, and a green skinny scarf. She's crossed her arms and is smiling. She's also wearing her black prosthetic limbs and black sneakers.

## Speaking Engagements and Disability Consulting
Eman is available to come speak on panels, at conference and as a Disability Consultant for your projects.
She has spoken at conference with City And State, Non-Profit Con, the University of Michigan, Riders Alliance, DC Comics, Skybound and Fordham University to name a few.
