## An Artists Journey
Since Eman's father gave her a Fuji Film camera when she was 11, in 1995, she has been committed and dedicated to art of all kinds.
From taking hundreds of thousands of photographs over the years, to writing thousands of poems, stories, and articles, to creating hundreds of drawings, paintings, and mixed media pieces, she expresses her soul's journey in the world, and shares that with anyone interested in her artistic expression.
Feel free to reach out for any submissions, collaborations, projects, or artistic adventures that you think she may be interested in!
