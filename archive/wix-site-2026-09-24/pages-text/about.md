# Meet our Team!

###### Eman Rimawi-Doster
Founder, Executive Director
*Disability Justice Advocate* Speaker * Educator * Writer *
Eman Rimawi-Doster is a Black and Palestinian woman, born and raised in NYC. For 26 years, she’s been organizing around diversity, equity, and inclusion in disenfranchised communities, while using art, writing, fashion, creativity, and organizing. She learned the ins and outs of taking action and having integrity from her parents. She has made it her life’s mission to change the things that need a push in a more inclusive direction. Her father wanted her to change the things she saw wrong and she does with everything she touches.
She went on to teach creative writing, community organizing, and political science workshops to youth in the city. She’s been focused on organizing disability rights and the intersectionality of it within every aspect of life.
She is committed to educating people on the interconnectedness of disability within multiple communities. Life doesn’t end when you have a disability. She began Diversity Includes Disability, a consulting firm designed to support and educate organizations and businesses on disability equity, while she continues to be a Disability Consultant for D.C. Comics and Skybound, and speaks at conferences about equity and inclusion for all people with disabilities.
Eman is also the funniest person you’ll ever meet.

###### Bryanna Copeland
Chair of the Board

###### Bryanna Copeland holds a Masters of Healthcare Administration and a Masters of Social Work from from the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai and Long Island University, respectively. She works in the Mount Sinai Health Neurology Department as the System Programs Coordinator. Having become an amputee herself, Bryanna enjoys being a mentor and advocating for other amputees via peer mentoring with disability organizations. Bryanna has also worked to ensure all individuals are provided equal access to public transportation. Aside from her work and volunteering commitments, she loves to rock climb and play tennis.
Leonard Blades is a CUNY alumnus of Brooklyn College with a B.A in Psychology and a graduate of the CUNY School of Professional Studies (SPS) where he received both his Master's Degree in Disability Services in Higher Education and the Advanced Certificate in Disability Studies. He has served as the Chairperson for the CUNY Coalition for Students with Disabilities, the Vice Chair for Disability Affairs for CUNY's University Student Senate, He is also the founder of CUNY SPS' Abilities and Resources Committee, now known as the Disability and Access Coalition.
At Queensborough Community College, he works as a Disability Accommodations Specialist for the Office of Accessibility Services and is currently a member of the MTA’s Advisory Committee for Transit Accessibility. Leonard’s motto in life is that while you may encounter defeat, you should never truly feel defeated.
